# Learning Journey

### Build a consistent learning habit, one day at a time.

Learning Journey is an iOS app that helps users build consistency in learning by setting a learning goal, tracking daily progress, and maintaining a streak. Users can record each day as Learned or use a limited Freeze to protect their progress when they need a break.

## Features

- **Learning Goals:** Create a learning goal and choose its duration: one week, one month, or one year.
- **Daily Tracking:** Mark each day as Learned or Freeze.
- **Flexible Daily Selection:** Change your daily selection throughout the day before it is finalized.
- **Freeze Allowance:** Use a limited number of Freeze days based on the selected goal duration.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor your learning progress and streak.
- **Calendar View:** View your daily activity and track your progress over time.
- **Goal Completion:** Review your results when the selected goal duration ends.
- **Offline Support:** Track your learning progress without requiring an internet connection.
